Skill Tree privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Without an account

You can use Skill Tree without signing in. Your finished skills, your level and your training days are saved in your own browser. We do not see them.

If you sign in with Google

Google gives us your name, your email address and your profile picture. We use them only to know which progress is yours and to show who is signed in.

We save your progress in your account: the skills you finished, your level and step, the days you trained, and your training streak. This lets you see the same progress on every device.

Who stores your data

Your account and progress are stored by Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States. The app itself is hosted by Vercel. Google handles the sign-in.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not share your data with anyone else.

Who can see your progress

Only you. Each account can read and change only its own progress.

Deleting your data

Email gjinistefan00@gmail.com and we will delete your account. Deleting your account deletes all of your saved progress.
