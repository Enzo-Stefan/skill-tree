hapi skill privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Without an account

You can use hapi skill without signing in. Your finished skills, your level and step, the days you trained and your training streak are saved in your own browser. So are the weights you log in the Strength tree. We do not see them.

If you sign in with Google

Google gives us your name, your email address and your profile picture. We use them only to know which progress is yours and to show who is signed in.

We save your progress in your account: the skills you finished, your level and step, the days you trained, and your training streak. This lets you see the same progress on every device.

The weights you log in the Strength tree are not saved in your account. They stay in the browser on that device, even when you are signed in.

Age

Accounts are for people 13 and over. Younger players can use hapi skill without signing in.

Who handles your data

We use a few other companies to run the app. Each one only gets what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase stores your account and progress, on servers in the United States.
  • Google handles signing in.
  • Vercel hosts the app.
  • YouTube only gets data when you tap play on a skill's video, or tap a link to YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube before that.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see your progress

Only you, and the two of us who run the app when we need to fix something. Each account can read and change only its own progress.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself in the app: open Settings, tap your name, then Delete account. It deletes your account and all of the progress saved in it, on every device. It can't be undone.

Progress saved in your browser stays there until you use Reset everything in Settings or clear your browser's data. It clears the tree you have open, so use it on each tree to clear everything.

If you can't sign in anymore, email enzo.abuliak@gmail.com or gjinistefan00@gmail.com and we will delete your account for you.
