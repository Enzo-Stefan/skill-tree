Skill Tree privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Without an account

You can use Skill Tree without signing in. Your finished skills, your level and your training days are saved in your own browser. We do not see them.

If you sign in with Google

Google gives us your name, your email address and your profile picture. We use them only to know which progress is yours and to show who is signed in.

We save your progress in your account: the skills you finished, your level and step, the days you trained, and your training streak. This lets you see the same progress on every device.

Age

If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian before signing in.

Who handles your data

We use a few other companies to run the app. Each one only gets what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase stores your account and progress, on servers in the United States.
  • Google handles signing in.
  • Vercel hosts the app.
  • YouTube only gets data if you tap a link or video to watch a tutorial.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see your progress

Only you, and the two of us who run the app when we need to fix something. Each account can read and change only its own progress.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account.
